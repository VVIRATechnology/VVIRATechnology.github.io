Building AI for Human Progress

VViraTech is an applied AI software company focused on two domains that matter most — education and healthcare. We build intelligent systems that improve how people learn and how people receive care, powered by research from our own AI lab.

  • Legendic – Our student learning & development platform. Personalised, interest-driven education powered by agentic AI.
  • Healistic – Our healthcare software platform. AI tools for wellbeing, care workflows, and patient support systems.
  • AImpact R&D – Our foundational AI research lab. The science engine behind both products, open to academia and industry.

Our Products

Two focused platforms built for the people who matter most — students and patients.

Legendic

Learning & development system for students and interest-based education.

Legendic is an adaptive AI platform that helps students discover their strengths, follow curated learning paths, and grow through interest-driven education — from AI classes for kids to professional upskilling for adults.

  • Personalised AI learning pathways
  • Interest-based class discovery
  • Skill assessment & progress tracking
  • Agentic AI tutoring & smart recommendations
  • Agentic AI classes for kids & adults

Healistic

Health software for wellbeing, care workflows, and patient support systems.

Healistic is an intelligent healthcare software platform that streamlines clinical operations, supports patient wellbeing, and empowers care teams with AI-driven insights — reducing friction so clinicians can focus on care.

  • Hospital workflow & task automation
  • Patient triaging & care coordination
  • Wellbeing monitoring & support tools
  • Clinical data management & analytics
  • AI-assisted decision support for clinicians

Research Foundation

The science engine powering both products — open to academia and industry.

AImpact R&D

Fundamental AI research lab — foundational methods with real-world impact systems.

AImpact R&D is a multidisciplinary research lab dedicated to advancing the boundaries of artificial intelligence. We work on foundational AI methods — from EEG/ESI signal analysis and biosignal foundation models to agentic architectures and representation learning — then translate that science directly into Legendic and Healistic.

The lab is open to collaboration with universities, hospitals, and industry partners.
