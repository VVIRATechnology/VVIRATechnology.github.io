MINDOS

Our Intelligent Health Guardian.

MINDOS is our intelligent healthcare platform, designed to help people better understand, manage, and protect their health. Currently, we focus on brain health and neurological diseases, with plans to expand into other areas of healthcare.

  • Proactive, personalised health guidance
  • Continuous wellbeing monitoring
  • Intelligent health insights and early risk awareness
  • Support for patients, caregivers, and healthcare professionals
  • Secure and responsible handling of health information
  • Intelligent EEG and ESI analytics with NEUROII, compatible with most EEG systems
