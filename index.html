Building AI for Real-World Progress

  • Legendic – A personalised learning and student development platform, powered by agentic AI and adaptive learning systems.

Our Services

We create intelligent platforms that transform learning and healthcare experiences through AI-driven innovation.

Legendic

Learning & development system for students and interest-based education.

Legendic is an adaptive AI platform that helps students discover their strengths, follow curated learning paths, and grow through interest-driven education, from AI classes for kids to professional upskilling for adults.

  • Personalised AI learning pathways, assessment, and progress tracking
  • Interest-based class discovery and AI classes for kids and adults
  • Club and society platform for learning communities and collaboration
  • Organization of events, competitions, and hackathons
  • Certification and leaderboards

Handora

Health software for wellbeing, care workflows, and patient support systems.

Handora is an intelligent healthcare software platform that streamlines clinical operations, supports patient wellbeing, and empowers care teams with AI-driven insights, reducing friction so clinicians can focus on care.

  • Tailored hospital workflow and task automation
  • Patient triaging and care coordination support
  • Assistive wellbeing monitoring and support tools
  • Secure clinical data management and advanced AI analytics
  • AI-assisted decision support tools for clinicians
