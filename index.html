Our Services

We create intelligent platforms that transform learning and healthcare experiences through AI-driven innovation.

Legendic

Learning & development system for students and interest-based education.

Legendic is an adaptive AI platform that helps students discover their strengths, follow curated learning paths, and grow through interest-driven education, from AI classes for kids to professional upskilling for adults.

  • Personalised AI learning pathways
  • Interest-based class discovery
  • Skill assessment & progress tracking
  • Agentic AI tutoring & smart recommendations
  • Agentic AI classes for kids & adults

Healistic

Health software for wellbeing, care workflows, and patient support systems.

Healistic is an intelligent healthcare software platform that streamlines clinical operations, supports patient wellbeing, and empowers care teams with AI-driven insights, reducing friction so clinicians can focus on care.

  • Hospital workflow & task automation
  • Patient triaging & care coordination
  • Wellbeing monitoring & support tools
  • Clinical data management & analytics
  • AI-assisted decision support for clinicians

Research Foundation

The science engine powering our products, built for collaboration across academia and industry.

AImpact R&D

Foundational AI research lab bridging scientific discovery and real-world AI systems.

AImpact R&D is a multidisciplinary AI research lab focused on translational research — advancing fundamental understanding of intelligence while building real-world systems for business and enterprise applications.

Our work spans multimodal AI and its applications, efficient agentic AI adaptation, safety and security in agentic systems, and enterprise-grade agentic AI workflows. We develop foundational methods and translate them into deployable solutions for education and healthcare through Legendic and Healistic.

Our AImpact Lab collaborate with academia through AImplifier R&D, enabling joint innovation across research and deployment.
